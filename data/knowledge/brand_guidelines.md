# Brightloop Brand Guidelines

Brightloop is a fictional B2B marketing analytics SaaS used as the demo company for CampaignPilot.

## Voice and tone
Brightloop sounds like a smart, friendly analyst: clear, confident and practical. We explain value in concrete outcomes (time saved, revenue tracked, reports automated) rather than hype. Write in plain English at roughly a grade 8 reading level. Use the second person ("you") and active voice.

## Words we never use
Never use these words or phrases in any campaign copy: "revolutionary", "game-changer", "guaranteed", "best in the world", "100% free", "act now", "limited time only!!!", "click here". Never use more than one exclamation mark in a message. Never write subject lines in ALL CAPS.

## Claims and compliance
Do not promise specific results such as "double your revenue". You may cite our published customer average: teams save about 6 hours per week on reporting. Every email must include an unsubscribe line. Only contact customers who have opted in to email (email_opt_in = 1). Discounts must state when they end.

## Personalisation
Use the token {{first_name}} to personalise greetings. Personalised subject lines have historically lifted open rates, so prefer them for email. Do not reference sensitive attributes such as age in copy.

## Channel rules
Email: subject line at most 60 characters, preview text at most 90 characters, body 60 to 180 words, exactly one primary call to action.
Social (LinkedIn, Instagram): post text at most 220 words for LinkedIn and 120 words for Instagram, up to 3 relevant hashtags, no hashtags in the middle of sentences.
Ads (Google, Meta): headline at most 30 characters, description at most 90 characters, call to action from: "Start free trial", "Book a demo", "See pricing", "Learn more".

## Calls to action
Preferred CTAs: "Start free trial", "Book a demo", "See your dashboard", "Upgrade to Pro", "Join the webinar".
