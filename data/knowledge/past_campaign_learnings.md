# Learnings from Past Campaigns

## Email
Personalised subject lines that include {{first_name}} consistently outperformed generic subjects on open rate. The welcome series and win-back emails performed best when they gave one clear next step instead of a feature list. Emails that led with a specific outcome ("save 6 hours a week") got more clicks than emails that led with a feature name.

## Win-back
Customers inactive for 60 to 120 days respond to "what's new" messaging plus a reason to log in, such as a ready-made dashboard. Customers inactive for more than 180 days rarely convert; keep the message short and low-pressure.

## Upgrades
Free users who were active in the last 14 days are the best upgrade audience. The strongest upgrade hook is automated AI weekly reports, followed by connecting more ad platforms.

## Regional and seasonal
Festive campaigns for India (Diwali) and the UAE (Ramadan, with productivity framing rather than discounts) performed above the email average. Send times between 9 and 11 am local time worked best.

## Social and ads
LinkedIn case studies with a concrete number in the hook drove the most qualified clicks for agencies. Google Ads retargeting free users had a low click-through rate but the best return on ad spend among ad campaigns. Short headlines with the product outcome beat headlines with the brand name.
