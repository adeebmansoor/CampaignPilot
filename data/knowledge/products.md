# Brightloop Products and Pricing

## Free plan
Price: $0. One connected data source (Google Analytics or a CSV upload), 3 dashboards, weekly email summary. Best for individuals trying the product.

## Pro plan
Price: $29 per user per month, or $290 per user per year (two months free). Unlimited dashboards, up to 10 data sources including Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn and HubSpot, automated AI-written weekly reports, scheduled exports, and Slack alerts when a metric drops. The most popular upgrade reason is automated AI reports.

## Business plan
Price: $99 per user per month, or $990 per user per year. Everything in Pro plus unlimited data sources, multi-touch attribution, custom AI report templates, SSO, role-based access control, and a dedicated success manager. Built for agencies and teams managing many clients.

## Key features
AI Reports: Brightloop writes a plain-English summary of what changed in your marketing metrics each week and why.
Attribution: see which channel (email, social, paid ads) actually drove a conversion.
Anomaly alerts: get notified in Slack or email when spend spikes or conversions drop.
Client workspaces (Business): agencies can manage each client in a separate workspace with white-labelled reports.

## Current offers
Annual plans include two months free. Students and educators get 50% off Pro with a valid school email. Agencies on Business get a free onboarding workshop.
